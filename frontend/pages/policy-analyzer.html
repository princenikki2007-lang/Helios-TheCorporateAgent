Worth reviewing Annual leave & working hours

Your policy mentions annual leave, but the applicable requirement under the state establishment rules may have changed for overtime compensations.

Current rule: Karnataka Shops & Commercial Establishments Act mandates double wages for hours worked beyond 9 hours/day or 48 hours/week. Compensatory off alone is not compliant for non-managerial staff.
Worth reviewing POSH Committee External Member

Your policy constitutes an Internal Complaints Committee, but does not explicitly name an active external member from an NGO or legal background.

Current rule: POSH Act 2013 Section 4(2)(c) mandates that at least one member of the Internal Committee be from an NGO or familiar with workplace harassment prevention.
