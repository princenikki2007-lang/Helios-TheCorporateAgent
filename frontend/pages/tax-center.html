⚠️ High Priority Tax Compliance Advisory: CBIC Notification 14/2026

Under Notification No. 14/2026-Central Tax, businesses with aggregate turnover exceeding ₹2 Crores must generate Invoice Reference Numbers (IRN) and QR codes for all B2B supplies. Additionally, under Rule 88C, if the tax liability reported in Form GSTR-1 exceeds the liability discharged in Form GSTR-3B by more than 10%, Form GSTR-1B reconciliation notice is generated automatically, requiring immediate response within 7 days.

Statutory Citation: Rule 48(4) & Rule 88C of CGST Rules 2017
