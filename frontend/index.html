04 · QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding HELIOS regulatory coverage and document handling.

Does HELIOS provide formal legal advice?

No. HELIOS is a compliance intelligence and legal decision-support system. It extracts facts, flags discrepancies, and cites official gazette notifications. All high-stakes transactions should be reviewed by your qualified corporate counsel.

What file formats does the Document Vault support?

HELIOS natively ingests PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, PNG, JPG, and scanned certificates up to 25MB per file with automated optical character recognition (OCR).

How does the Company Impact Engine filter regulatory updates?

When a new official notification is published by MCA, CBIC, or MeitY, HELIOS correlates your entity type, state jurisdiction, turnover bracket, employee headcount, and existing policy clauses to assign an actionable High, Medium, or Low relevance score.
